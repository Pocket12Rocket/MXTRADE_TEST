# Legal & PayFast Compliance Research — Fast Sport

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Repo:** MXTRADE_TEST ("Fast Sport" — South African dirt-bike gear/parts/accessories marketplace)
**Status:** Research draft only.

> **DISCLAIMER — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This document was produced by an AI research agent reading public PayFast/legal-firm web pages and the app's source code. It is a starting checklist, not a legal opinion. Before going live with a real PayFast merchant account or onboarding real sellers/buyers, this must be reviewed and confirmed by (a) a South African attorney familiar with the ECT Act, CPA and POPIA, and (b) PayFast merchant support / the PayFast onboarding team directly. Do not treat any "Status" or "Priority" below as final without that review.

## 2. PayFast go-live checklist

Based on PayFast's public Internet Merchant Requirements and support docs (not the confidential due-diligence checklist PayFast uses internally — confirm exact requirements with PayFast support before going live):

1. **Website content** — Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund/Service Policy and Shipping Policy must all be published and linked from the Home page and the Checkout page. *(Currently: none of these are reachable — see LEGAL-01 to LEGAL-04, LEGAL-29.)*
2. **Payment method branding** — the PayFast logo / accepted-payment-method icons should appear on the Home page and Checkout page. *(Currently missing — LEGAL-07.)*
3. **Contact & business details** — up-to-date local contact details and a physical business address must be visible. *(Currently missing — LEGAL-09, LEGAL-13, LEGAL-22.)*
4. **Site security** — SSL/HTTPS and general security hygiene (PayFast doesn't require the merchant to be PCI-DSS certified itself, since PayFast hosts the actual card-entry page, but the merchant site should be served over HTTPS). *(Believed fine on Firebase App Hosting — confirm in production.)*
5. **ITN/IPN handling** — the notify handler must (a) verify the notification actually came from PayFast (PayFast's `validate` endpoint call, or IP allow-listing), (b) verify the payment amount matches the order, (c) verify the payment data is well-formed. *(Implemented for (b); implemented via server-to-server validation for (a) but **disabled by default when `PAYFAST_SANDBOX=true`** — must confirm this flag is `false` in production before going live, see LEGAL-10.)*
6. **Sandbox → Live account transition** — PayFast requires a business verification/due-diligence process (company documents, banking details, authorised signatory ID) before a live (non-sandbox) merchant account is activated. *(Owner-level task, not code — LEGAL-11.)*
7. **Prohibited/restricted goods** — PayFast (like most South African PSPs) maintains a restricted merchant category list. Confirm dirt-bike gear/parts/accessories (including any safety equipment such as helmets) are not restricted, and that "used"/second-hand goods don't trigger extra requirements.
8. **Marketplace/aggregator model question** — PayFast does support multi-vendor marketplaces via its **Split Payments** feature, which instantly splits an incoming payment between the platform and seller accounts at the point of payment. **Fast Sport's current design does not use Split Payments** — instead, the full amount goes to the platform's single PayFast merchant account, and the platform manually holds funds and pays sellers out later per the seller T&C ("Payments made by customers will be held by FastSport until the purchased product has been successfully delivered..."). This "hold-and-pay-later" pattern is a materially different risk/compliance profile than PayFast's native split model (it makes FastSport look more like a funds-holding intermediary), and should be discussed directly with PayFast merchant support to confirm it is permitted under their merchant agreement, or whether adopting Split Payments (or a formal escrow arrangement) is required. This also interacts with the FICA question in LEGAL-23.

## 3. Open questions for the owner

These cannot be answered from the codebase and need the business owner's input before the legal pages/checklist above can be finalized:

1. **Legal entity** — What is the registered legal/trading name, and is there a CIPC company registration number to disclose (LEGAL-13, LEGAL-22)?
2. **VAT status** — Is the operating entity VAT-registered? If so, what is the VAT number, and are listed/checkout prices meant to be VAT-inclusive (LEGAL-16)?
3. **Responsible party for goods** — When a product is defective, counterfeit, or never arrives, who is legally and contractually responsible to the buyer: FastSport (the platform) or the individual seller? The seller T&C (`components/TermsAndConditionsModal.js`) implies sellers bear authenticity/condition responsibility while FastSport controls delivery and payment release — this split needs to be reflected consistently in a buyer-facing policy too.
4. **New vs used goods** — Are items sold on Fast Sport new, used, or both? This directly determines whether the Second-Hand Goods Act applies (LEGAL-21) and what warranty language is appropriate.
5. **Funds flow** — Does FastSport intend to keep holding buyer funds until delivery and manually pay sellers (as the seller T&C currently states), or move to PayFast's native Split Payments? This affects the FICA question (LEGAL-23) and the PayFast merchant-agreement question (see checklist item 8).
6. **Physical address** — What address should be displayed publicly (registered office / trading address) to satisfy ECT Act s43 and PayFast requirements (LEGAL-09, LEGAL-13)?
7. **Information Officer** — Has an Information Officer been registered with the Information Regulator, and who should be named as the POPIA contact (LEGAL-17, LEGAL-18)?
8. **Marketing emails** — Are newsletter/marketing emails planned? If so, an opt-in consent flow and unsubscribe mechanism will need to be built (LEGAL-27); if not, this can stay low priority.
9. **Data residency** — Can the owner (or whoever manages the Firebase project `mx-trade-163ce`) confirm the actual region of the Firebase Storage bucket and Firebase Auth data, since only Firestore is explicitly pinned to `africa-south1` in `firebase.json` (LEGAL-20)?
10. **Seller identity verification** — Is a typed ID number sufficient for the business's risk appetite, or should ID document upload + verification be added, particularly if the Second-Hand Goods Act or FICA is found to apply (LEGAL-21, LEGAL-23)?
